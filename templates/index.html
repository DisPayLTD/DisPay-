Platform capabilities

A single system of record for company payments.

DisPay is designed for finance and HR teams that need payroll, statutory remittance, and vendor payments to run reliably — and equally usable by individuals who need to send multiple payments at once, with a verifiable record for every transaction.

⚡

Real-time disbursement

Payments settle in real time, removing the multi-day delays typical of conventional bank transfers.

💼

Scheduled payroll runs

Configure your pay cycle once. DisPay executes payroll automatically each period, without manual initiation.

🧾

Automated receipts & audit trail

Every transaction generates a receipt sent directly to the recipient, supporting reconciliation and internal record-keeping.

💡

Utility payments

Settle recurring company utilities, including electricity and water, from the same dashboard used for payroll.

🧮

PAYE remittance

Route statutory PAYE deductions accurately and on schedule, reducing compliance exposure for your finance team.

📤

Bulk payments

Upload a CSV or spreadsheet and disburse to any number of recipients in one run — available to individual users and businesses alike, not just full payroll accounts.

📄

Vendor & bill payments

Process vendor invoices and one-off company payments through a single, trackable workflow.

Transparent, predictable pricing

A flat ₦20 charge applies per transaction, regardless of amount, allowing finance teams to forecast payment costs with certainty.
